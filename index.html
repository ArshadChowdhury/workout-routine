Training block · 5 days/week

Push /
Pull

A 5-day push/pull hitting every trainable muscle group 2x per week — including traps, forearms, glute medius, rotator cuff, and rear delts that most 5-day programs skip. Exercises are grouped by synergy within each day so muscles that work together get trained together. Abs on 4 of 5 days.

Muscle Coverage · Everything 2x/Week

  • Chest (pec major upper + lower): Sat (bench, incline DB) + Wed (incline BB, chest press, close-grip) = 2x
  • Front Delts: Sat (bench, DB press) + Wed (OHP, incline BB) = 2x
  • Side Delts: Sat (cable lateral) + Wed (DB lateral) = 2x
  • Rear Delts: Sun (face pull) + Wed (reverse pec deck) = 2x
  • Triceps (all 3 heads): Sat (pushdown) + Wed (close-grip + rope OH for long head) = 2x
  • Traps (upper + mid + lower): Sun (shrug + face pull) + Wed (OHP indirect) + Thu (row) = 2x+
  • Lats: Sun (row, pull-up) + Thu (pull-up, CSR, straight-arm PD) = 2x
  • Rhomboids/Mid Back: Sun (row, face pull) + Thu (CSR) = 2x
  • Biceps: Sun (BB curl, hammer) + Thu (preacher curl) = 2x
  • Brachialis: Sun (hammer curl) + Thu (preacher, hammer indirect) = 2x
  • Forearm Flexors: Sun (all pulls, hammer curl grip) + Thu (all pulls) = 2x indirect + direct
  • Forearm Extensors: Thu (reverse wrist curl) = 1x direct (rarely trained — this is enough)
  • Serratus Anterior: Sat (bench with proper scap protraction) + Thu (straight-arm PD) = 2x
  • Rotator Cuff: Sun (face pull) + Wed (reverse pec deck) = 2x
  • Erector Spinae: Tue (squat, RDL) + Thu (single-leg RDL) = 2x
  • Quads: Sat (front squat/lunge) + Tue (back squat, split squat) = 2x
  • Hamstrings: Tue (RDL, leg curl, split squat) + Thu (single-leg RDL) = 2x
  • Glute Max: Tue (squat, RDL, split squat) + Thu (single-leg RDL) = 2x
  • Glute Medius/Minimus: Tue (hip abduction, split squat) + Thu (single-leg RDL stabilization) = 2x
  • Adductors: Tue (squat, Bulgarian split squat) = 1x compound
  • Calves (Gastrocnemius): Tue (standing calf) + Sun/Wed (indirect through squat/lunge) = 2x
  • Calves (Soleus): Thu (seated calf) = 1x direct (bent-knee position is what isolates it)
  • Hip Flexors: Sat (hanging leg raise) + Tue (hanging knee raise) = 2x
  • Abs (Rectus + Transverse + Obliques): Sat + Sun + Wed + Thu = 4x with variety

Progression Rules

  • Compounds (bench, squat, OHP, RDL, row, front squat): add 2.5 kg when you hit the top of the rep range on all working sets with clean form.
  • Isolations: add reps first — once you hold the top of the range for 2 sessions, then bump the weight.
  • New exercises (front squat, single-leg RDL, hip abduction, reverse wrist curl, straight-arm pulldown): start light for 2–3 weeks. Nail form before chasing load.
  • If you've been progressing on the U/L split, carry those working weights forward for shared movements.
  • Log every session — weight × reps × RPE. Progressive overload in a deficit is a game of small weekly wins, not big jumps.

Cut-Phase Notes

  • Sat and Thu run longer (~75–80 min) because of the extra leg work. If late sets tank on those days, drop the last isolation before dropping the compound.
  • Sat and Tue both hit quads. Keep Saturday's front squat/lunge moderate (RPE 7), save the intensity for Tuesday's main squat.
  • Tue and Thu both hit posterior chain. Tue's RDL is heavy; Thu's single-leg RDL is lighter and mostly unilateral — don't try to match Tue's load.
  • If your lower back feels beaten up mid-week, swap Thu's single-leg RDL for Barbell Hip Thrust (3 × 10 @ 40 kg) — still hits glutes/hams, no spinal load.
  • Volume is higher than the U/L split (5 sessions, ~40 exercises/week). If sleep or recovery slips 2 weeks running, drop the last isolation from each day.
  • Optional finisher (max 2×/week): 8–10 min incline walk after training. Skip intervals — recovery is already stressed by the deficit.

Substitutions & Notes

  • Front Squat not comfortable? Sub DB Walking Lunge (3 × 10/leg @ 6 kg each).
  • No hip abduction machine? Banded lateral walks (3 × 20 steps/direction) or side-lying leg raises hit the same muscle.
  • No reverse pec deck? DB reverse fly (on flat bench, chest-down) or cable reverse fly (arms crossed at low pulley) both work.
  • No T-bar row? Chest-supported machine row or DB row on an incline bench.
  • Weighted Pull-up too hard? Assisted machine or bands until you can hit 3 × 6 clean at bodyweight.
