Return ramp (first 4 weeks)

  • Weeks 1–2: 2 sets of every exercise, RPE 6–7 (stop with 3–4 reps left). Tendons and joints adapt slower than muscle after a layoff.
  • Weeks 3–4: full sets as written, RPE 7–8.
  • Week 5+: RPE 8–9 on compounds. Isolations can go to 1 rep short of failure.

Weekly frequency

  • Chest: Sat (flat bench, incline DB, pec deck) + Wed (incline BB, cable fly)
  • Shoulders: Sat (Arnold, laterals) + Wed (OHP, cable laterals, reverse pec deck) + Sun (face pull)
  • Triceps: Sat (close-grip, overhead extension) + Wed (pushdown)
  • Back: Sun (wide pulldown, BB row, chest-supported row) + Thu (neutral pulldown, cable row, DB row)
  • Biceps: Sun (incline DB, Bayesian — outer head) + Thu (EZ curl, hammer)
  • Forearms: Sun (reverse curl, wrist curl) + Thu (hammer, wrist curl)
  • Traps: Sun (DB shrug) + Thu (BB shrug)
  • Legs: Mon only — squat, RDL, leg press, extension, leg curl, hip thrust, calves. Don't skip Mondays.
  • Total: 40 exercises/week (8 + 9 + 8 + 7 + 8), sessions ~70–90 min with warm-up.

Progression

  • Compounds: add 2.5 kg once every set hits the top of the rep range with clean form.
  • Machines + isolations: add reps first. Hold the top of the range for 2 sessions, then add one weight step.
  • Log every session (weight × reps). On a cut, small weekly wins are the goal.

Safety + recovery

  • Breathe every rep on squats, RDLs and rows. Holding your breath across reps is what triggered your exertion headaches before.
  • Squat + RDL on the same day is the hardest part of the week. Keep both at RPE 7 until week 5.
  • Elbows: pull days carry a lot of curling. If the inside or outside of the elbow aches, drop the forearm block for a week first.
  • You have 2 hours, but don't fill them. Results level off after ~90 min, and extra volume on a cut mostly eats recovery.
  • Sat → Sun → Mon is three days straight. Keep Tue as a true rest day.
  • Nutrition: protein at 140 g every day, including rest days. Slightly more rice on Mon (leg day).
  • Drop to 4 days if two of these last 2+ weeks: lifts stalling, poor sleep, joint aches that don't fade overnight, dreading sessions. Remove Push B (Wed) first.

Substitutions

  • Arnold Press feels awkward: Seated DB Shoulder Press, 10 kg each.
  • Bayesian station busy: an extra set of Incline DB Curls.
  • No Reverse Pec Deck: Face Pull or bent-over DB reverse fly.
  • Lower back tired on RDL: make Hip Thrust 4 × 8 and swap RDL for Cable Pull-Through 3 × 12.
  • Knee acts up: drop Leg Extension first, then swap Back Squat for Goblet Squat.
  • Wrist curls bother your wrists: Farmer's Carry 3 × 30–40 m with heavy DBs.
