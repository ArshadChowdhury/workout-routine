Why This Split Structure

  • Sat + Sun pair naturally as your weekend push/pull block — you're fresh Saturday, and different muscles Sunday means minimal overlap.
  • Mon rest lets both push and pull recover before Tue's dedicated leg day.
  • Tue Legs sits between rest days — you're recovered coming in, and Wed's upper day doesn't compete with leg recovery.
  • Wed Upper (priority) is your highest-volume session, hitting every upper body muscle a second time that week — Fri + Sat off afterward makes room for it.
  • Legs 1x/week is the trade-off for a dedicated leg day. Less frequency but higher concentrated volume — this works well for legs specifically because they recover slower than upper body anyway.

Muscle Coverage

  • Chest: Sat (Smith Bench, Incline DB, Pec Deck) + Wed (Incline BB, Cable Fly) = 2x
  • Shoulders (front): Sat (DB Press) + Wed (OHP) = 2x
  • Shoulders (side): Sat (Lateral Raise) + Wed (Lateral Raise) = 2x
  • Rear Delts + Rotator Cuff: Sun (Face Pull) + Wed (Face Pull) = 2x
  • Triceps: Sat (Close-Grip, Rope Ext) + Wed (Pushdown) = 2x
  • Back: Sun (Pull-up, BB Row, CSR) + Wed (Pull-up, T-Bar Row) = 2x
  • Traps: Sun (Shrug) = 1x direct + indirect from rows
  • Biceps: Sun (BB Curl, Hammer Curl, Machine Curl) + Wed (Preacher Curl) = 2x with four total exposures
  • Quads: Tue (Squat, Leg Press, Leg Extension) = 1x with three exposures
  • Hamstrings: Tue (RDL, Lying Leg Curl) = 1x with concentrated volume
  • Glutes: Tue (Squat, RDL, Pull-Through) = 1x with concentrated volume
  • Calves: Tue (Standing Calf Raise) = 1x direct
  • Abs: Sat + Sun + Tue + Wed = 4x with variety (Hanging Leg Raise, Cable Crunch, Hanging Knee Raise, Plank)

Progression Rules

  • Compounds (Smith Bench, OHP, RDL, BB Row, Pull-up, Squat): add 2.5 kg when you hit the top of the rep range on all working sets with clean form.
  • Machines & isolations (Pec Deck, Cable Fly, Leg Curl, Leg Extension, Pull-Through, Face Pull, all curls, pushdowns): add reps first — once you hold the top of the range for 2 sessions, then bump the weight one increment.
  • Log every session — weight × reps × RPE. Progressive overload in a deficit is a game of small weekly wins.

Cut-Phase Notes

  • Total volume: ~35 exercises/week (8 + 9 + 8 + 10). Higher than a standard 4-day plan but front-loaded toward the rest days.
  • Sat + Sun back-to-back — respect the intra-workout rest periods. Sun's back work will feel heavier because of Sat's shoulder fatigue; that's normal.
  • Wed is your hardest day — don't try to PR the compounds on Wed. Match the working weights from Sat/Sun's equivalent lifts.
  • Knee still recovering? Sub Leg Extension for Bulgarian Split Squat (3 × 10/leg, moderate depth). Leg extension is one of the worst exercises for patellofemoral pain.
  • If Wed consistently ruins Thu recovery, drop the last exercise from each block (D and E) — Pushdown, Plank. That gets you to 8 exercises without losing anything critical.
  • Optional finisher (max 2×/week): 8–10 min incline walk after training. Only after Sat or Sun, never after Wed.

Substitutions

  • Ready to move to barbell bench? Once shoulders feel resilient (week 6–8+), replace Smith Machine Bench Press with Barbell Bench Press on Sat.
  • Goblet Squat instead of Back Squat? Sub in during weeks 1–4 or if knee is still healing. Progress to back squat when knee is 100%.
  • Knee still sore on Tue? Skip Back Squat AND Leg Extension. Do Leg Press twice (once heavy 5×6-8, once volume 3×10-12) plus Bulgarian Split Squat 3×10/leg.
  • Lower back bothered by RDL? Swap for Barbell Hip Thrust or Cable Pull-Through — both hit glutes/hams without the spinal load.
  • No T-bar row? Chest-supported DB row on incline bench works.
  • No Cable Fly setup? DB Fly on flat or incline bench works, though slightly more shoulder stress.
  • Weighted Pull-up too hard? Assisted machine or bands until 3 × 6 clean at bodyweight.
