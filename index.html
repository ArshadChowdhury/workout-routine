Training block · 5 days · Push A · Pull A · Legs · Push B · Pull B

Split /
Block

A 5-day split with one solid leg day (Mon) and four upper body days paired as Push A / Pull A / Push B / Pull B. Every upper body muscle hits 2x per week with different emphasis on each day — Sat chest-focused, Wed shoulder-focused; Sun back-thickness + outer biceps, Thu back-width + arm compounds.

Why Push A / Push B and Pull A / Pull B

  • Each muscle 2x per week is the highest-return training frequency in the research (Schoenfeld 2016, Ralston 2018 meta-analyses). Once per week works but adding a second exposure grows muscle faster, especially on a cut.
  • A/B lets you emphasize different exercises without losing volume. Sat push is chest-heavy (3 chest exercises); Wed push is shoulder-heavy (OHP as the main compound). Both hit chest and shoulders, but the emphasis shifts — each muscle group gets its best exercise once and its "backup" once.
  • Sun and Thu pull days follow the same pattern. Sun leads with BB Row (back thickness) and long-head-focused biceps; Thu leads with Pull-up (back width) and compound BB Curl + Hammer Curl.
  • Legs stays 1x/week on Mon as a solid, complete session. Squat + RDL + Leg Press + Leg Extension + Leg Curl + Pull-Through + Calves. Enough volume that one session covers weekly leg needs during a cut.
  • Sat/Sun/Mon three-day block works because each day hits totally different muscle groups (push → pull → legs). No muscular overlap, only systemic fatigue — and Tue rest handles that.
  • Tue and Fri rest stay non-negotiable. Fri is gym-closed anyway; Tue is the recovery buffer between the 3-day block and the Wed/Thu double.

Muscle Coverage

  • Chest: Sat (Smith/BB Bench, Incline DB, Pec Deck) + Wed (Incline BB, Cable Fly) = 2x with 5 total exposures
  • Shoulders (front): Sat (Arnold Press) + Wed (Standing OHP) = 2x with different modalities
  • Shoulders (side): Sat (DB/Cable Lateral Raise) + Wed (Cable Lateral Raise) = 2x
  • Rear Delts + Rotator Cuff: Sun (Face Pull) + Wed (Face Pull) = 2x
  • Triceps: Sat (Close-Grip, Rope Ext) + Wed (Pushdown) = 2x with 3 total exposures
  • Back: Sun (Lat Pulldown, BB Row, CSR) + Thu (Pull-up, Cable Row, DB Row) = 2x with 6 total row/pulldown exposures, different angles on each day
  • Traps: Sun (DB Shrug) + Thu (BB Shrug) = 2x with different variations
  • Biceps (long head/outer focus): Sun (Incline DB Curl, Bayesian Cable Curl) = 2 outer-focused exposures + Thu (BB Curl heavy compound) = solid outer emphasis fixing the inner-head dominance
  • Brachialis + Forearms: Thu (Hammer Curl) = 1x direct — pushes biceps up as it grows, adds arm width from the side view
  • Quads: Mon (Squat, Leg Press, Leg Extension) = 1x with 3 exposures
  • Hamstrings: Mon (RDL, Lying Leg Curl) = 1x with 2 exposures
  • Glutes: Mon (Squat, RDL, Cable Pull-Through) = 1x with 3 exposures
  • Calves: Mon (Standing Calf Raise) = 1x direct
  • Abs: Sat + Sun + Mon + Wed + Thu = 5x with variety (Hanging Leg Raise, Cable Crunch, Hanging Knee Raise, Plank, Cable Crunch)

Progression Rules

  • Compounds (Smith/BB Bench, Arnold, OHP, RDL, BB Row, Lat Pulldown, Squat): add 2.5 kg when you hit the top of the rep range on all working sets with clean form.
  • Machines & isolations: add reps first — once you hold the top of the range for 2 sessions, then bump the weight one increment.
  • Log every session — weight × reps × RPE. Progressive overload in a deficit is a game of small weekly wins.

Fixing the Biceps Imbalance

  • The problem with preacher-heavy training: the preacher pad puts your elbow in front of the body, which shortens the long head of the biceps (the outer/lateral head). Result: the short head (inner) gets hammered every set while the long head barely stretches — inner grows, outer stays flat, arm looks narrow from the front.
  • The fix — train with elbows behind or at the body so the long head is fully stretched. Incline DB Curl and Bayesian Cable Curl both do this; they're arguably the two best long-head builders that exist.
  • Incline DB Curl is the classic king — full stretch at the bottom, hard eccentric. Expect to use much less weight than standing curls (that stretch is brutal).
  • Bayesian Cable Curl keeps constant tension throughout the entire ROM, which the DB version loses at the top. Best done one arm at a time so you can really focus.
  • Hammer Curl doesn't hit the biceps directly, but the brachialis sits underneath the biceps — as it grows, it pushes the biceps up and out. You'll see arm width increase from the side view.
  • Give it 8–12 weeks before you judge — muscle growth pattern shifts don't show up week 1. Track a mirror photo now for comparison.

Cut-Phase Notes (5-Day Recovery Watch)

  • Total volume: ~38 exercises/week (8 + 8 + 8 + 7 + 7). Moderate for 5 days — the A/B pairing lets each session stay under 80 min while covering everything.
  • Three-day training block Sat→Sun→Mon is the highest systemic load point. Muscular overlap is minimal (push → pull → legs), but total nervous system fatigue accumulates. Tue rest is essential — do not train through it.
  • Watch for warning signs (any two consistently = drop to 4-day): joint stiffness that doesn't resolve overnight, motivation dropping mid-week, sleep quality dipping, compound weights plateauing for 2+ weeks, morning HR elevated 10+ bpm.
  • Sat + Sun back-to-back — Sun's back work will feel heavier because of Sat's shoulder fatigue. Normal. Keep BB Row weight matched to your 4-day version, don't try to add load in the first few weeks.
  • Wed OHP + Incline BB back-to-back is heavy on the front delts. Match Sat's working weights, don't chase PRs on Wed.
  • Knee still recovering? Skip Leg Extension on Mon. Leg extension is one of the worst exercises for patellofemoral pain. Reverse Lunge is a much safer sub.
  • Fall-back to 4-day if recovery slips: drop Push B (Wed) entirely and keep Sat/Sun/Mon/Thu. You go from 5-day back to 4-day PPPL with Wed and Fri as rest days.
  • Optional finisher (max 2×/week): 8–10 min incline walk after training. Only after Sat or Sun, never after Mon legs or Wed/Thu.

Substitutions

  • Smith vs Barbell Bench — either works on Sat. Alternate week to week if you want both stimuli.
  • Arnold Press too tricky? Fall back to Seated DB Shoulder Press (10 kg each) on Sat.
  • Goblet Squat instead of Back Squat? Sub in during weeks 1–4 or if knee is still healing. Progress to back squat when knee is 100%.
  • Knee still sore on Mon? Skip Back Squat AND Leg Extension. Do Leg Press twice + Reverse Lunge 3×10/leg.
  • Lower back bothered by RDL? Swap for Barbell Hip Thrust as the main hinge — hits glutes/hams heavily without spinal load.
  • Headache signs on RDL? Same fix as above — Hip Thrust as main hinge. Breathe every rep on any hinge variation.
  • No Cable Fly setup on Wed? DB Fly on flat or incline bench works, though slightly more shoulder stress.
  • No T-bar row on Sun? Chest-supported DB row on incline bench works.
  • Bayesian Cable Curl station busy? Do a second set of Incline DB Curls in its place — you'll get long-head work either way. Or standing DB curl with elbows pulled slightly back (10–15° behind torso) mimics the same stretch angle.
