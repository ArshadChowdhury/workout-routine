Progression Rules

  • Compounds (bench, squat, row, RDL, OHP, deadlift): add 2.5 kg when you hit the top of the rep range on all working sets with clean form.
  • Isolations (curls, extensions, calves, machines): add reps first — once you hold the top of the range for 2 sessions, then bump the weight.
  • Weeks 1–2 are form and neural drive — don't chase weight. If a session feels too light, resist adding load; earn it with clean reps first.
  • Log every session — weight × reps × RPE. Progressive overload in a deficit is a game of small weekly wins, not big jumps.

Cut-Phase Notes

  • If a compound feels 1–2 reps weaker two sessions in a row, check sleep, protein, and calories before blaming the program.
  • Rest periods on compounds are non-negotiable — you're training for muscle retention, not sweating.
  • Optional finisher (max 2×/week): 8–10 min incline walk after training. Skip intervals — recovery is already stressed by the deficit.
  • Schedule alternative — swap Sat → Wed if you want weekends fully free. Both work; Mon/Tue/Thu/Sat gives cleaner recovery spacing.
