Why 140g Protein, Not 170g

  • Protein needs scale with lean mass, not total weight. Fat mass doesn't need feeding — muscle does. Calculating on estimated LBM (~70 kg) instead of scale weight (91 kg) gives a more accurate target.
  • 2 g per kg LBM = 140 g/day — within the well-established muscle-preservation range for cutting athletes. Enough to protect muscle, without wasting calories on excess.
  • As you lean out and LBM changes, recalculate. If you drop fat and gain back muscle, LBM might climb toward 72–75 kg; nudge protein up to 145–150 g at that point.

Prep Tips

  • Air-fry chicken in bulk: ~500g twice a week is enough at this protein target — Sunday and Wednesday work well.
  • Boil 6 eggs at once and keep them in the fridge for quick breakfast prep.
  • Yogurt in 1kg tubs is dramatically cheaper than small cups. Portion into two 250 g servings per day.
  • Weigh rice cooked, not raw. 130g cooked ≈ 40g raw. Rice is the biggest source of accidental calories.
  • Cook with 1–2 tsp oil per meal, max. Use mustard or sunflower oil sparingly; skip ghee during the cut.

Swaps & Substitutions

  • Chicken → Soya chunks — same protein at 30% of the cost. Cook 40 g dry (rehydrates to ~120 g) in a light gravy.
  • Meal 4 nuts → 1 boiled egg — if you'd rather trade fat for extra protein (adds ~6 g P, saves ~50 kcal).
  • Rui → Small fish (taki, punti, tilapia) — cheaper, similar protein, more variety.
  • Rice → Chapati for dinner — slightly lower calories, higher protein per gram.
  • Milk → Soya milk if you're lactose sensitive; similar protein without the fat.
  • No time to cook? Meal 2 or 4 can flex to just 300 g yogurt + a fruit.

Cut-Phase Notes

  • Consistency > perfection. Hitting 130 g protein reliably every day beats 150 g four days and 90 g on the other three.
  • Weigh yourself once a week, same day, same time (morning, post-toilet, before eating). Look at the trend across 3 weeks — not daily fluctuations.
  • Cheat meals: 1 non-tracked meal per week is fine. Not a cheat day — a cheat meal. Get back on the plan the next meal.
  • Pre-workout: Meal 4 (yogurt + nuts) 1–2 hours before training works well. Or black coffee + a banana 30 min before if you train early.
  • Post-workout: The next scheduled meal is enough. Whey protein is optional, not essential — save the money if the budget is tight.
  • No zero-calorie drinks with meals (colas, sweetened tea). Water, black coffee, plain tea only. Zero-sugar sodas okay if you must.
