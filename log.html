Tracking Rhythm

  • Steps: daily. Log at the end of the day when the count is final. Missing a day breaks the streak visualization but doesn't break the plan.
  • Weight: weekly. Same day, same time — morning, post-toilet, before eating. Daily fluctuations mean nothing; the 3-week trend does.
  • Don't chase perfect numbers. A "bad" step day or a weight blip means nothing on its own. Only patterns across 3+ weeks are worth acting on.
  • When the trend stalls for 3 weeks straight, that's the signal to adjust — usually a small step goal bump or a 100–150 kcal calorie drop, not a full plan overhaul.

Data & Reset

  • All data is stored locally in this browser — no account, no cloud sync. Open it from the same browser and device each time.
  • To edit an entry, add a new one for the same date — it overwrites. Click the × next to any row to delete.
